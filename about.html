About the Hub

Why C2P Hub exists, and how it fits inside the Department of Computer Engineering.

Our Story

C2P Hub started as an informal group of Computer Engineering students who wanted more hands on building alongside coursework. It has grown into a structured space within the department where members pick a track, get mentorship, and ship real prototypes, from embedded devices to computer vision applications.

Where We're Based

Ground Floor, Department of Computer Engineering Building, ABU Zaria, alongside the department's MK, Control, and Smart Labs, so members can move from an idea to a working prototype without leaving the building.
