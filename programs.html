Innovation Programs

Tracks We Run

Pick a track and build toward a capstone project with mentorship along the way.

1 Track

AI & Data Science Track

A guided track covering Python, machine learning fundamentals, and computer vision, with a capstone model or application built by each participant.

2 Track

Embedded Systems & IoT Track

Hands on work with microcontrollers, sensors, and connected devices, from breadboard prototypes to functioning IoT nodes.

3 Track

Product Design & Prototyping

A short programme on turning a rough idea into a testable prototype: problem framing, quick builds, and peer feedback loops.

4 Track

Startup Incubation Programme

For teams with a working prototype who want to explore taking it further, light mentorship, pitch practice, and demo day slots.
